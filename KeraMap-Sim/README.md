# Keratoconus repair simulation

An interactive, browser-based illustration with three tabs: a repair scenario simulator, a
local protein delivery animation and a simulated vision comparison. Open `index.html`; no
installation is needed. The vision images and reading scores are not clinical tests or
surgical results.

**Illustrative only.** All shapes, material values and protein parameters are assumed. There is
no patient or laboratory data. It does not show that any protein or treatment works, and it is
not for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

## Simplifications

- Linear shallow-shell mechanics, not a finite element model.
- Thin-element optics with geometric blur.
- One-dimensional depth transport with assumed surface permeability and binding.
- Microneedles are an assumed starting deposit; insertion injury and mechanics are not modelled,
  and doses are not matched with the gel.
- The 3D eye is a rendered model, with the corneal shape difference magnified.
